What is Wych Hunt?

Wych Hunt is a fantasy tabletop skirmish game revolving around groups of individuals known as Hunters. These Hunters are made up of Warriors, Wyches, and many other types of denisens. The Hunting Parties seek to claim or destroy powerful magic items located deep in the Veiled Woods. Lead your Hunters to glory and death, pushing back the darkness, or ushering it forth.


Current Factions

Border Baronies

The peoples who live on the outskirts of the Empire. Due to the proximity to the Veiled Woods the risk is high, but the rampant growth of plants it provides is necessary to the survival of many people beyond the Baronies. While they don't wield arcane magics, their garrisons are well equipped to handle the threats that crawl from the dark.
