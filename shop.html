Backer Packages

Supporter tiers available for purchase. Higher tiers offer more content than the previous tiers. Supporter tier is for those who like the idea but don't want to invest in the game. Champion tier gives 1 faction of buyer's choice and everything needed to play. Captain tier gives both factions and everything needed to play.

Package Price Details
Supporter $5 Gives Border Baronies Night Watch Captain STL and stat card
Champion $40 Gives either Border Baronies or The Coven of Adanor STLs, the full list of stat cards for whatever you pick!
Captain $70 Gives the STL files for both Border Baronies and The Coven of Adanor factions and all of their stat cards.
